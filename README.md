# 🎯🐂 BullSheet

> **No Ads. No Subscriptions. Pure Darts.**  
> *A free, touch-optimized, open-source darts scoreboard & party game app built for iPad, mobile, and desktop.*

---

## ✨ Features

- **📱 Touch & iPad First**: Large touch targets (52px+), zero double-tap lag, high-contrast numbers designed to be easily read from 8 feet away at the oche.
- **👥 1 to 8 Players Pass-and-Play**: Save your friends to the roster and jump straight into 1v1 head-to-head or multi-player party matches.
- **🤖 5 AI Dart Bots**:
  - 🤡 *The Bullshitter* (35 Avg — throws 26 every second turn)
  - 🍺 *Pub Regular Dave* (52 Avg — steady pub league thrower)
  - 📊 *Clinical Accountant* (74 Avg — precise arithmetic)
  - 🎯 *Oche Master Jack* (89 Avg — lethal doubles)
  - 👑 *The 180 Machine* (104 Avg — world championship level)
- **🎮 7 Complete Game Modes**:
  1. **501 / X01**: (101, 301, 501, 701) with Straight In/Double In, Double/Single/Master Out, Legs & Sets, and Dynamic Checkout Matrix (170 down to 2).
  2. **Cricket & Cutthroat Cricket**: Visual slash `/`, cross `✕`, and closed `⨂` marks with live Marks Per Round (MPR).
  3. **Killer**: 2–8 players, hunt down each other's lives once you qualify as a Killer.
  4. **Elimination (Knockout)**: Beat the previous player's 3-dart score or lose a strike!
  5. **Split Score (Halve-It)**: Round targets (15, 16, Doubles, 17, 18, Trebles, 19, 20, Bull) — miss all 3 darts and your score is cut in half!
  6. **Shanghai**: Rounds 1–7 (or 1–20) with instant **Shanghai Victory** if you hit Single, Double, and Treble in one turn.
  7. **Around the Clock**: Solo and multi-player race from 1 to 20 + Bullseye.
- **🔢 Dual Input Modes**:
  - **Quick Score Keypad**: Enter turn total or tap preset buttons (`26`, `41`, `45`, `60`, `85`, `100`, `140`, `180!`).
  - **Interactive SVG Dartboard**: Tap exact segments (Single, Double, Treble, Bull) with animated landing markers.
- **🗣️ Audio & Voice Caller**:
  - Real-time procedural Web Audio effects (dart thud, bull gong, 180 fanfare chords, bust buzzer).
  - Web Speech synthesis voice caller (*"ONE HUNDRED AND EIGHTY!"*, *"Game shot and the match!"*, and sarcastic quips for 26 & busts).
- **🎨 4 Themes**:
  - 🐂 **BullSheet Pub Chalkboard** (Authentic charcoal & brass)
  - 📊 **Corporate Excel Parody** (Disguised spreadsheet mode to play at work)
  - ⚡ **PDC Broadcast Arena** (Electric cyan & purple)
  - 🌑 **OLED Midnight** (Pure minimalist high-contrast black)
- **🎲 Excuse Generator**: Over 30 instant excuses when you bust or throw a terrible 26.
- **🔌 100% Offline PWA**: Install to your iPad Home Screen and play anywhere without an internet connection.

## 🚀 1-Click Free Hosting (GitHub Pages)

You can host this for \$0 with zero server setup:

1. Push this repository to GitHub:
   ```bash
   git add .
   git commit -m "Initial commit of BullSheet Darts app"
   git push origin main
   ```
2. Go to your GitHub repo's **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Pages** (on the left sidebar).
3. Under **Branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)` folder, then click **Save**.
4. GitHub will give you a free live URL:  
   `https://<your-username>.github.io/bull-sheet/`

---

## 📱 How to Install on iPad / iPhone

1. Open your GitHub Pages link in **Safari** on your iPad.
2. Tap the **Share button** (square with an upward arrow).
3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
4. Tap **Add**.

The **BullSheet** app will now appear on your home screen with its custom icon and open full-screen (without browser bars), working 100% offline!
