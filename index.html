About BullSheet

BullSheet is a 100% free, open-source darts scoreboard licensed under GNU GPL v3.0. Designed specifically for iPad and mobile touchscreens with zero ads, zero trackers, and complete offline PWA capability.

To install on iPad: Open Safari → Tap Share (square with arrow) → Tap "Add to Home Screen".
